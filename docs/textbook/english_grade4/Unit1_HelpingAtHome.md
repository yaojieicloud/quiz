## 2. 第 4-5 页：Section A — 家庭与职业

### 章节标识
- **Section A**
- **How are families different?**

### Let's talk（对话练习）
```
Boy:  You have a big family. Is this your father?
Girl: Yes, it is. He's a PE teacher.
Boy:  What's your mother's job?
Girl: She's a doctor.
Boy:  Doctors are great! My father is a doctor too.
```

### Draw and say
```
Boy:  What's your mother's job?
Girl: She's a teacher.
```

### Let's learn — 单词表（家庭职业）
| 单词 | 中文释义 |
|---|---|
| farmer | 农民 |
| nurse | 护士 |
| doctor | 医生 |
| office worker | 办公室职员 |
| factory worker | 工厂工人 |

### Let's learn — 小对话
```
Boy:  What's your father's job?
Girl: He's a factory worker.
```

### Match and say
```
Girl: What's Mike's job?
Boy:  He's a doctor.
```

### 关键句型（Section A）
- **Is this your father?** → Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.
- **What's your mother's/father's job?** → She's/He's a [职业].
- **Doctors are great!** （感叹句）
- **My father is a doctor too.** （too 表"也"）

### 句型结构
- 询问职业：`What's your father's job?`
- 回答职业：`He/She is a/an + 职业`
- 询问身份：`Is this your father?` → `Yes, it is.`

## 6. 第 12-13 页：Reading time — 故事

### 标题
- **Reading time**
- 故事主题：**My Mum**（我的妈妈）

### 完整故事文本

```
① My mum is a writer.
   She writes a lot of good books.

② Mum is also a great cook.
   She can cook great food!

③ Mum is very busy.
   We can help her at home.

④ We want to make this.

⑤ But now we have this!

⑥ Mum is still happy!
   She's a great mum!
```

- Mum is also a great cook.

- Mum is still happy! She's a great mum!

## 7. 关键知识汇总（用于出题）

### 字母组合 ch /tʃ/
- 教学词汇：lunch, much, China, Chinese, chair, child, kitchen, teacher, chores, grandparents
- 教学句子：My grandpa can teach Chinese.

### 家庭职业词汇（Section A）
- farmer, nurse, doctor, office worker, factory worker, cook
- 句型：What's your father's job? — He is a farmer.
- 句型：Is this your father? — Yes, it is.

### 家务劳动词汇（Section B）
- sweep the floor 扫地
- clean the room 打扫房间
- cook 做饭
- wash the dishes 洗碗
- do some chores 做家务
- look after 照顾
- sister, family, together, happy, often, busy, tired, easy, great

### 情态动词 can 句型（核心）
